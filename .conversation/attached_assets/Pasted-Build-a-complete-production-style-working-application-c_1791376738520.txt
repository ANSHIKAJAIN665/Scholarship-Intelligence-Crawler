3. OFFICIAL SOURCE VERIFICATION
==================================================

This is one of the most important parts.

A scholarship is NOT verified merely because an AI model found it.

For every scholarship determine:

- Is the source official?
- Is the scholarship currently present on the official source?
- Is there an official application URL?
- Is eligibility supported by source evidence?
- Is the deadline supported by source evidence?
- Is the information current?
- Are there conflicting sources?
- Can important fields be traced to source evidence?

Official sources include:

- Government websites
- Official ministry/department websites
- Official university websites
- Official organization/provider websites
- Official scholarship portals

Aggregators/blogs/articles can be used for discovery only.

They must NOT be stored as the authoritative source.

crawler/verifier.py
